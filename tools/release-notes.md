People Playground in 11 languages: English, 简体中文, Español, Français, Português (Brasil), Русский, Deutsch, 日本語, Türkçe, 한국어, Polski. Pick the language in the main menu.

**x64** - Steam and almost every other copy. **x86** - only the 32-bit game.
Install: INSTALL.txt inside the archive (INSTALL_RU.txt in Russian).
